Update the website from a CV

Reads a CV, compares it with what the site already publishes, and writes the differences you approve into the site’s data files. Everything happens in this browser — nothing is uploaded, and your CV never leaves the computer. The original files are not modified; you download new copies.

1Choose the CV

Click or drop BZCV….docx or ….pdf here

A Word .docx reads more accurately than a PDF, because Word keeps the real headings, bold and italic, and link addresses. Use the PDF only if that is all you have.

2Review the differences

3Download the updated data files
